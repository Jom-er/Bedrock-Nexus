Add-on Installation Guide

Follow these three simple steps to enhance your game with any Bedrock Nexus download!

  1. 01

    Download the Add-on File (.mcaddon)

    Locate the download link on our site. The files are usually a single file with the extension .mcaddon (or .mcpack for resource packs). Save it to a location on your device, like your Downloads folder.

  2. 02

    Open the File to Import

    On most devices (Windows 10+, Mobile), simply tap or double-click the downloaded file. This action will automatically launch Minecraft Bedrock Edition and begin the internal import process.

  3. 03

    Activate in Your World Settings

    Once the import succeeds, edit your existing or new Minecraft world. Go to the "Resource Packs" and/or "Behavior Packs" sections under the Add-Ons tab and activate the newly imported pack to apply it to your world.
